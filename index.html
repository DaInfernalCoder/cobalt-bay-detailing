05 / QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Clear details make for a smoother mobile appointment.

How do I get started?

Call 832-924-3725 or book online. Share the vehicle type, its condition, and whether you are considering interior, exterior, or complete detailing.

Do you come to the vehicle?

Yes. Cobalt Bay is a mobile car detailing service for Houston. When scheduling, confirm the location and any access details so the appointment can be planned correctly.

How long will detailing take?

Timing depends on the vehicle, condition, and agreed scope. Share those details when you call or book so the appointment can be planned around the actual work.

What should I do before the appointment?

Remove valuables and personal items you need to keep. Confirm the vehicle location and discuss any specific stains, surfaces, or exterior areas you want addressed.

Can I ask about a specific concern?

Yes. Describe the concern before scheduling so it can be considered when discussing the service. Some conditions may need an in-person look before the scope is clear.
