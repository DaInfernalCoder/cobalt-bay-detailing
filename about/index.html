Start with the vehicle’s needs

A useful detailing request starts with the part of the vehicle you want to improve. Interior vacuuming addresses accessible loose debris; seat shampooing and steam cleaning are discussed for suitable materials. Exterior washing addresses ordinary surface dirt. Waxing, clay treatment, and headlight polishing are separate choices to confirm in the quote.

Tell Richmond Car Detailing what you see, what has already been tried, and which areas matter most. The service pages explain preparation, likely boundaries, and questions to ask before choosing the work.

Make the appointment clear

Describe the vehicle, its condition, and the result you are seeking. Ask which tasks are included, which are separate, and whether existing damage or a previous treatment limits suitability. Confirm the quote, appointment setting, and any preparation before the work begins.

If the vehicle will be needed soon afterward, mention that during booking. Moisture-based interior treatments, site access, and weather can affect the practical plan; a service name alone does not establish a fixed duration or drying time.
