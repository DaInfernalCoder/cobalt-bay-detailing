The service

What to discuss before booking

Richmond Car Detailing lists Auto interior vacuuming, Steam cleaning, Seat shampooing, and Interior scenting. Confirm which of these services belong in your appointment and whether the vehicle is suitable.

  • Vacuuming accessible seats, carpets, floor mats, footwells, crevices, and cargo areas.
  • Discussing steam cleaning or seat shampooing where the surface material and condition are suitable.
  • Choosing the cabin areas that need attention before the appointment starts.
  • Optional interior scenting, discussed separately from cleaning an odor source.

When this service fits

Choose interior detailing when the cabin is the main concern: crumbs, tracked-in dirt, dusty surfaces, or suitable seat and carpet buildup. A focused interior request helps explain which areas matter most. If both the cabin and exterior need attention, discuss the two services together so the quote covers the work you actually want.

Before the visit

Prepare the vehicle and location

Remove valuables, loose personal items, chargers, and as much cargo as practical so the areas to be cleaned are accessible. Discuss child seats and any item that should stay in place.

Point out tears, loose trim, electronics, delicate materials, old stains, pet hair, and previous cleaning treatments. Tell the business when you need to use the vehicle again, especially if shampooing or another moisture-based method is being considered.

Know the limits

Cleaning cannot reverse worn fabric, damaged trim, permanent discoloration, or every stain and odor. Steam and shampoo are considered only for suitable materials. A routine detailing request should not be treated as mold remediation, biohazard treatment, or mechanical repair; describe those concerns before booking so the appropriate next step can be discussed.

A useful first decision

Seats and trim need different decisions

Cloth, leather, carpet, hard trim, screens, and electronic accessories should not be treated as one identical surface. Tell the business about damaged material, aftermarket accessories, and any manufacturer care instructions you already follow. A treatment appropriate for one area may be unsuitable for another. The useful decision is which method fits the surface and condition, rather than assuming steam or shampoo belongs everywhere.

Plan around the next use of the vehicle

Tell the business if the vehicle is needed for a school run, work shift, or other fixed commitment after the appointment. If moisture is used, drying depends on the treatment, material, and conditions at the actual location. Do not assume a fixed drying time from the service name. Discuss the usable areas and timing before agreeing to a fabric-cleaning treatment.

Appointment planning

Agree on the work before it starts

Describe the vehicle, the condition, and the areas you want addressed. Confirm the appointment location, access, and any utility requirements. Ask for the included work and quote before booking, then review the agreed areas and any limitations at the end of the visit.

Questions before booking

Interior Car Detailing FAQs

What can interior detailing include?

The menu includes interior vacuuming, steam cleaning, seat shampooing, and interior scenting. The exact combination depends on the vehicle, the material, and the scope agreed before the visit; each method is not automatically included in every request.

Can every stain or odor be removed?

No result should be promised without assessing the material and cause. Old stains, prior treatments, permanent discoloration, and an inaccessible odor source can limit what cleaning achieves.

Should I empty the vehicle first?

Remove valuables and as many loose belongings as practical. Discuss child seats, cargo, and items that need to stay in place so access and priorities are clear.

Is steam cleaning suitable for every surface?

No. Steam is considered only for suitable materials and conditions. Tell the business about electronics, delicate trim, existing damage, and previous treatments before a method is agreed.

Can interior work be combined with an exterior service?

Interior and exterior services are both on the menu. Ask for a combined quote and confirm the included work rather than assuming an exterior wash is part of every interior appointment.

Does interior scenting remove the source of an odor?

A fragrance can change how the cabin smells, but it is not evidence that the source was removed. Describe the odor and its history so cleaning and any limits can be discussed first.
