Ask about add-ons separately

The menu also lists waxing, clay bar treatment, engine detailing, paint repair, and window tinting. These need their own scope and suitability discussion. A routine wash should not imply paint repair, and wax should not be treated as ceramic coating or permanent protection.

If the vehicle needs both cabin and exterior care, describe the priorities in each area and request a combined quote. Confirm which items are included rather than assuming every listed service belongs to one package.

Useful details for a quote

  • Vehicle type and the condition of the areas needing attention.
  • The service or combination of services you are considering.
  • Known damage, films, coatings, previous treatment, or persistent marks.
  • The actual appointment location, parking permission, access, and any utility restrictions.
